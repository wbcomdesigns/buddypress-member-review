## Thought Leadership

### The Trust Problem
```
Online communities have a trust problem.

When someone joins your BuddyPress community, they see a member directory — profiles with photos and bios. But they can't answer the most important question:

Who can I trust?

Without reputation signals:
• Skilled members look the same as beginners
• Reliable people blend in with unreliable ones
• Members hesitate to hire, collaborate, or connect

We built BuddyPress Member Reviews to solve this.

It adds member-to-member reviews — star ratings, written feedback, and verified badges — right on profiles.

Now your community answers the trust question automatically.

Used by 10,000+ BuddyPress communities.

Free on WordPress.org. Pro for testimonials, endorsements, and API access.

#CommunityBuilding #WordPress #BuddyPress
```

### Star Ratings Don't Fit Every Community
```
Star ratings work great for restaurants. They don't always fit professional communities.

"4.5 stars" makes sense for a delivery driver. It feels weird for rating a colleague's leadership skills.

That's why we built three review modes:

𝗥𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲 — Traditional 5-star reviews with customizable criteria. Perfect for marketplaces where buyers rate sellers.

𝗧𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗼𝗻𝗶𝗮𝗹𝘀 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲 — Text-only feedback without stars. Perfect for course platforms where students share success stories.

𝗘𝗻𝗱𝗼𝗿𝘀𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁𝘀 𝗠𝗼𝗱𝗲 — Skill vouching, LinkedIn-style. Perfect for professional networks where members endorse each other's expertise.

Same plugin. Different presentation. Choose what fits your community.

BuddyPress Member Reviews Pro — $69/year
```

### Community Moderation at Scale
```
The hardest part of running a community platform isn't getting content.

It's moderating it.

When your BuddyPress community grows past 1,000 members, you can't read every review manually. You need systems.

Here's what we built into Member Reviews:

𝟭. 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝘆 𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴
Any member can flag an inappropriate review. You define the reasons: spam, harassment, conflict of interest.

𝟮. 𝗔𝘂𝘁𝗼-𝗵𝗶𝗱𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗵𝗼𝗹𝗱
After X reports (you choose), reviews automatically hide. No admin action required until review.

𝟯. 𝗕𝘂𝗹𝗸 𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀
Process multiple reviews at once. Approve, delete, or dismiss reports in batches.

𝟰. 𝗢𝘄𝗻𝗲𝗿 𝗿𝗲𝗽𝗹𝗶𝗲𝘀
Let profile owners respond once to reviews. Dialogue often resolves issues without admin intervention.

Your community helps police itself. You handle edge cases.

This is in the free version. No paywall.

#CommunityManagement #WordPress #Moderation
```
