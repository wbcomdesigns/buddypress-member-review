## Visual Guidelines

**Image style:**
- Clean screenshots from the plugin
- White or light backgrounds
- No stock photos
- Annotations where helpful

**Carousel covers:**
- Bold text
- Plugin accent colors
- Clear hierarchy

**Reels:**
- Quick cuts
- Text overlays
- Upbeat but not overwhelming music
