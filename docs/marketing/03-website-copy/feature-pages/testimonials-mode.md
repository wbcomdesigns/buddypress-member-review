# Testimonials Mode

**Pro Feature** — Text-only reviews without star ratings.

---

## The Problem

Star ratings feel wrong for some communities.

When someone completes your course, transforms their business with your coaching, or achieves a goal through your membership — reducing that to "4.5 stars" misses the point.

Numbers can't capture:
- The specific breakthrough moment
- What changed in their life or career
- Why they'd recommend you to others

---

## The Solution

Testimonials Mode removes star ratings entirely. Members share experiences in their own words.

Instead of:
```
★★★★★ (5.0)
"Great course!"
```

You get:
```
"Sarah's marketing course helped me land my first three clients within a month.
The Facebook Ads module alone paid for the entire program."
— Maria, Freelance Designer
```

---

## How It Works

1. Enable Testimonials Mode in Pro settings
2. Tab label changes from "Reviews" to "Testimonials"
3. Star rating fields disappear from the form
4. Profile header hides aggregate rating
5. Members submit text testimonials only

**Your existing rating data isn't deleted.** Switch back to Ratings mode anytime and everything reappears.

---

## Best For

### Online Course Platforms
Students share success stories, specific results, and transformation moments. More persuasive than anonymous star counts.

### Coaching & Consulting Sites
Clients describe their experience working with you. Trust comes from relatable stories, not numbers.

### Membership Communities
Members recommend each other based on real interactions. Text testimonials feel more genuine than ratings.

### Service Professionals
Doctors, therapists, trainers — industries where star ratings feel transactional or inappropriate.

---

## What Changes in Testimonials Mode

| Element | Ratings Mode | Testimonials Mode |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| Tab label | "Reviews" | "Testimonials" |
| Form fields | Stars + Text | Text only |
| Profile header | Shows average + count | Hidden |
| Criteria ratings | Displayed | Hidden |
| Activity posts | Include stars | Text only |

---

## Why Testimonials Convert Better

**For course creators specifically:**

Star ratings invite comparison. "Why is this course 4.6 when that one is 4.8?"

Testimonials invite connection. "She had the same struggle I have. And it worked for her."

Studies show testimonials with specific outcomes outperform generic ratings for high-consideration purchases like courses and coaching.

## Enabling Testimonials Mode

1. Go to **Settings > Member Reviews > Pro**
2. Under Review Mode, select **Testimonials**
3. Save changes

That's it. The change is instant across your site.
