## WordPress.org Long Description

### Opening Paragraph
The BuddyPress Member Reviews plugin lets registered members rate and review each other's profiles. Build trust in your community by surfacing reliable, skilled, and helpful members through star ratings, written feedback, and reputation display.

### Feature Highlights

**Star Ratings**
Members rate each other on a 5-star scale. Aggregate ratings display on profile headers and in the member directory.

**Multiple Criteria**
Define custom rating criteria like "Communication," "Quality," or "Timeliness." Each criterion gets its own star rating.

**Written Reviews**
Optional text feedback accompanies star ratings. Members share specific experiences, not just numbers.

**Owner Replies**
Profile owners can respond to reviews once — thank positive reviewers or address concerns professionally.

**Review Reporting**
Community members report inappropriate reviews. Auto-hide reviews after reaching a configurable threshold.

**Moderation Queue**
Approve reviews before publishing. Admins control what appears on profiles.

**Top Members Shortcode**
Display your highest-rated members anywhere with `[bupr_display_top_members]`.

**Email & BP Notifications**
Members receive email and BuddyPress notifications when someone reviews their profile.

**Anonymous Option**
Let reviewers stay private when leaving feedback.

**Template Overrides**
Customize review display by copying templates to your theme's `/review/` folder.

### Compatibility
- WordPress 6.0+
- BuddyPress 12.0+
- BuddyBoss Platform
- Youzify

### Pro Addon Available
Upgrade to Pro for Testimonials Mode, Endorsements Mode, Verified Badges, Flexible Criteria, and REST API.
