## The Problem

Your BuddyPress community has a trust problem.

Members join and see profiles — photos, bios, maybe activity. But they can't answer:
- Who's reliable?
- Who's skilled?
- Who's worth hiring or connecting with?

Without reputation signals, good contributors go unrecognized, bad actors blend in, and members hesitate to engage.

---

## The Solution

BuddyPress Member Reviews adds trust signals to every profile.

**Star Ratings** — Members rate each other on customizable criteria (Communication, Quality, Reliability — you decide)

**Written Reviews** — Specific experiences, not just numbers

**Owner Replies** — Profile owners can respond once to reviews

**Report System** — Community flags inappropriate content

**Moderation Queue** — You control what gets published

---

## Three Review Modes (Pro)

| Mode | What Members See | Best For |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| **Ratings** | 5-star reviews with criteria | Marketplaces, service directories |
| **Testimonials** | Text-only feedback, no stars | Courses, coaching, memberships |
| **Endorsements** | LinkedIn-style skill badges | Professional networks, expert directories |
