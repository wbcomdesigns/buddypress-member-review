## Testimonial Questions

For interviews or detailed feedback:

### Setup & Implementation
1. How long did it take to set up Member Reviews?
2. What was the most confusing part (if any)?
3. Did you need support? How was the experience?

### Before/After
4. What were you using before Member Reviews?
5. How did members discover each other before reviews?
6. What changed after implementing reviews?

### Specific Value
7. What feature do you use most?
8. What feature surprised you (in a good way)?
9. Has any feature saved you time? How much?

### Results
10. Have reviews affected member engagement?
11. Any metrics you can share? (review count, conversion, support tickets)
12. Would you recommend it? To whom?

### Improvement
13. What's missing that you wish was included?
14. What would make the plugin 10x better?

## Testimonial Format Templates

### Short Format (for website)
> "[One sentence result]."
> — [First Name], [Community Type]

**Example:**
> "Reviews cut our support tickets by 40%."
> — Sarah, Marketplace Owner

---

### Medium Format (for landing page)
> "[Problem they had]. [How Member Reviews helped]. [Result achieved]."
> — [First Name], [Role], [Community Type]

**Example:**
> "Our course pages had star ratings that felt transactional. Testimonials Mode lets students share real success stories. Conversions improved immediately."
> — Mike, Founder, Online Course Platform

---

### Long Format (for case study)
**Community:** [Name/Type]
**Challenge:** [What they were struggling with]
**Solution:** [How they used Member Reviews]
**Results:** [Specific outcomes, metrics if available]
**Quote:** "[Their words]"
