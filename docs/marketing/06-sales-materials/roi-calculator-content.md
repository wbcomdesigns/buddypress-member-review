# ROI Calculator Content

Content for justifying the value of Member Reviews Pro.

**Important:** All statistics and projections are estimates based on typical customer implementations. Actual results vary based on community size, engagement level, and implementation.

---

## Value Framework

### The Trust Equation

```
Community Value = Members × Trust Level × Engagement

Low Trust → Low Engagement → Low Value
High Trust → High Engagement → High Value
```

Reviews increase trust, which increases engagement and community value.

## ROI Arguments by Use Case

### Marketplace ROI

**Problem Cost:**
- Support tickets from buyer-seller disputes
- Refunds from mismatched expectations
- Time spent mediating conflicts

**With Reviews:**
- Buyers self-select quality sellers
- Expectations set before transaction
- Disputes decrease

**Example Calculation:**
```
Before Reviews:
- 100 transactions/month
- 15% dispute rate = 15 disputes
- 30 min per dispute = 7.5 hours
- $50/hour support cost = $375/month

After Reviews:
- 100 transactions/month
- 9% dispute rate = 9 disputes (40% reduction)
- 30 min per dispute = 4.5 hours
- $50/hour support cost = $225/month

Monthly Savings: $150
Annual Savings: $1,800
Pro Cost: $69-299/year
ROI: 500-2,500%
```

## Opportunity Cost

### What You're Not Doing

Time spent building/maintaining a review system is time not spent on:
- Core product development
- Marketing and growth
- Community engagement
- Revenue-generating activities

**Example:**
- 150 hours building reviews
- 150 hours × $100/hour opportunity cost = $15,000
- OR: 150 hours on marketing = 150 blog posts, 50 videos, 1,000 social posts

## Risk Reduction

### Without a Review System
- Members leave for platforms with trust signals
- Quality contributors go unrecognized
- Bad actors damage community reputation
- Hiring/collaboration fails happen publicly

### With Reviews
- Trust signals retain members
- Contributors get recognized
- Community self-moderates
- Transactions happen with informed consent

**Risk Value:** Hard to quantify, but one major community incident can cost thousands in damage control.
