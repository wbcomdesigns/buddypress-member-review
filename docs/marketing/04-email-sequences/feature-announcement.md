## Example: Owner Replies Announcement

**Subject:** "New: Let profile owners respond to reviews"

### Body

Hi,

BuddyPress Member Reviews 3.7.0 includes a feature you've been requesting — Owner Replies.

**What it does:**
Profile owners can now respond once to each review they receive.

**Why it matters:**
Two-way communication builds trust. Owners can thank positive reviewers or address concerns professionally — publicly.

**How to enable it:**
1. Go to Settings > Member Reviews > General
2. Find "Enable Owner Replies"
3. Toggle to "Yes" and save

When someone reviews a member, that member sees a "Reply" button below the review.

**Who should use this:**
- Marketplace sellers responding to buyer feedback
- Course instructors thanking students
- Any community where dialogue matters

---

Update to 3.7.0 from your WordPress dashboard.

— Wbcom Designs Team

## Example: Report System Announcement

**Subject:** "New: Community-powered moderation is here"

### Body

Hi,

Version 3.7.0 adds a report system to BuddyPress Member Reviews.

**What it does:**
Any member can report an inappropriate review. After enough reports (you set the threshold), reviews are auto-hidden for admin review.

**Why it matters:**
Your community helps police itself. You spend less time moderating, and inappropriate content gets reported faster.

**How to configure:**
1. Settings > Member Reviews > General
2. Enable "Allow Review Reporting"
3. Set "Report Threshold" (default: 5)

When a review hits the threshold, it's hidden until you approve or delete it.

**Report reasons include:**
- Spam or fake review
- Harassment or abuse
- Inappropriate content
- Conflict of interest

---

Update now from Plugins > Updates.

— Wbcom Designs Team

## Segmentation

**Send to Free users:**
- Free feature announcements
- Compatibility updates
- Pro teasers in footer

**Send to Pro users:**
- All Pro feature announcements
- Free feature announcements (marked as "included in your license")
- Priority update notifications

**Suppress:**
- Users who unsubscribed from product updates
- Inactive sites (no logins in 90+ days)
