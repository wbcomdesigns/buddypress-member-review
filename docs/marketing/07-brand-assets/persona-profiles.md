# Target Personas

## Persona 1: Community Carl

### Demographics
- **Role:** Community Manager / Site Owner
- **Age:** 32-45
- **Team Size:** Solo or small team (1-5)
- **Industry:** Niche online communities (hobby, interest-based)
- **Technical Level:** Intermediate WordPress user

### Goals
- Build trust between community members
- Surface helpful/reliable members
- Reduce spam and bad actors
- Keep members engaged and returning

### Pain Points
- Members don't know who to trust
- No way to recognize helpful contributors
- Generic profiles don't show reputation
- Spam reviews on competitor platforms burned him before

### Why BuddyPress Member Reviews Appeals
- **Star ratings** show who's reliable at a glance
- **Moderation queue** prevents spam reviews
- **Report system** lets community self-police
- **Top members shortcode** highlights contributors

### Key Message
> "Finally, a way to show who's actually helpful in your community."

### Content They Respond To
- How-to guides
- Community building tips
- Case studies from similar communities
- Feature comparisons

## Persona 2: Marketplace Maya

### Demographics
- **Role:** Marketplace Owner / Founder
- **Age:** 28-40
- **Team Size:** 2-10 person startup
- **Industry:** Freelancer platforms, service marketplaces
- **Technical Level:** Comfortable with WordPress, uses developers for complex work

### Goals
- Build buyer confidence in sellers
- Reduce refund requests through better matching
- Create accountability for service providers
- Compete with established marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork)

### Pain Points
- Buyers can't verify seller quality before purchasing
- Bad experiences lead to chargebacks and support tickets
- Self-reported skills aren't trustworthy
- Generic review plugins don't integrate with BuddyPress profiles

### Why BuddyPress Member Reviews Appeals
- **Criteria-based ratings** (Quality, Communication, Timeliness)
- **Verified badges** mark proven transactions (Pro)
- **REST API** for mobile app integration (Pro)
- **Weighted criteria** prioritize what matters most (Pro)

### Key Message
> "Turn your BuddyPress community into a trusted marketplace."

### Content They Respond To
- ROI calculations (reduced refunds)
- Competitor comparisons
- API documentation
- Marketplace success stories

## Persona 3: Course Creator Kate

### Demographics
- **Role:** Online Course Creator / LMS Admin
- **Age:** 30-50
- **Team Size:** Solo entrepreneur or small team
- **Industry:** Online education, coaching, training
- **Technical Level:** Uses WordPress + LMS (LearnDash, LifterLMS)

### Goals
- Collect student testimonials automatically
- Show social proof on course pages
- Let instructors build reputation
- Increase course enrollments through trust

### Pain Points
- Star ratings feel too transactional for education
- Students forget to leave testimonials
- No central place to display success stories
- Generic testimonial plugins don't integrate with profiles

### Why BuddyPress Member Reviews Appeals
- **Testimonials Mode** — words over stars (Pro)
- **Verified badges** prove student-instructor relationship (Pro)
- **Owner replies** let instructors respond (Free)
- **Activity stream** posts show social proof

### Key Message
> "Student testimonials that actually convert."

### Content They Respond To
- Before/after enrollment stories
- Testimonial templates
- Integration guides (LearnDash + BuddyPress)
- Email sequence templates for requesting testimonials

- **Age:** 35-55
- **Team Size:** 5-25

- **Verified badges** mark real colleagues (Pro)
- **BuddyPress-native** means one unified profile

### Key Message

### Content They Respond To

- API documentation

- LinkedIn comparison content
