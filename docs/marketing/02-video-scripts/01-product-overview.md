# Product Overview Video Script

## Video Details
- **Title:** BuddyPress Member Reviews — Add Trust to Your Community
- **Duration:** 2:30
- **Purpose:** Introduce the plugin and demonstrate core value
- **Tone:** Friendly, professional, problem-solution focused
- **Target:** Community managers evaluating the plugin

---

## Script

### INTRO (0:00 - 0:15)

**VISUAL:** Member profile without any reputation signals

**NARRATOR:** "Your BuddyPress community has a trust problem. New members join and see profiles — but they can't tell who's reliable, who's skilled, or who's worth connecting with."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Who can you trust?"

---

### PROBLEM (0:15 - 0:30)

**VISUAL:** Generic member directory with identical-looking profiles

**NARRATOR:** "Without reputation signals, good contributors go unrecognized. Bad actors blend in. Members hesitate to hire, collaborate, or engage."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "No reviews = No trust signals"

---

### SOLUTION INTRO (0:30 - 0:45)

**VISUAL:** Profile with star rating badge and review count

**NARRATOR:** "BuddyPress Member Reviews changes that. It lets your members rate and review each other — with star ratings, written feedback, and trust signals right on their profiles."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "BuddyPress Member Reviews"

---

### FEATURE WALKTHROUGH (0:45 - 1:30)

**VISUAL:** Add Review form with star ratings

**NARRATOR:** "Members can rate each other on custom criteria — like Communication, Quality, or Reliability. Each rating builds toward an aggregate score."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Multiple Rating Criteria"

---

**VISUAL:** Review with written text and star ratings

**NARRATOR:** "Written reviews let members share specific experiences. Not just numbers — real feedback."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Written Reviews"

---

**VISUAL:** Profile owner reply under a review

**NARRATOR:** "Profile owners can respond once to reviews — thanking positive reviewers or addressing concerns professionally."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Owner Replies"

---

**VISUAL:** Report modal with reason options

**NARRATOR:** "If a review is inappropriate, anyone can report it. After enough reports, it's automatically hidden for admin review."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Community Moderation"

---

**VISUAL:** Top Members shortcode output

**NARRATOR:** "Showcase your highest-rated members anywhere with a simple shortcode."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "[bupr_display_top_members]"

---

### PRO TEASER (1:30 - 2:00)

**VISUAL:** Three mode icons (Stars, Quote, Skills)

**NARRATOR:** "Need more flexibility? The Pro addon offers three review modes."

---

**VISUAL:** Testimonials mode example

**NARRATOR:** "Testimonials Mode removes star ratings — perfect for courses and coaching sites where stories matter more than scores."

---

**VISUAL:** Endorsements mode with skill badges

**NARRATOR:** "Endorsements Mode adds LinkedIn-style skill vouching for professional networks."

---

**VISUAL:** Verified badge on a review

**NARRATOR:** "Plus verified badges, flexible criteria, and a full REST API."

---

### CTA (2:00 - 2:30)

**VISUAL:** WordPress.org plugin page

**NARRATOR:** "Get started free on WordPress.org. The free version includes everything you need — star ratings, moderation, notifications, and the Top Members shortcode."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Free on WordPress.org"

---

**VISUAL:** Wbcom Designs Pro page

**NARRATOR:** "Upgrade to Pro when you need Testimonials, Endorsements, or Verified Badges. Choose what fits your community."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "wbcomdesigns.com"

---

**VISUAL:** Plugin logo with tagline

**NARRATOR:** "BuddyPress Member Reviews. Build trust between members."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "Build trust between members."

## Production Notes

### Visuals Needed
- Screenshots from local dev environment (docs/images/)
- Screen recordings of:
  - Submitting a review
  - Owner reply flow
  - Report modal
  - Admin moderation queue

### Music
- Upbeat but not overwhelming
- Fade under during narration
- Corporate/professional feel

### Transitions
- Smooth fades between sections
- No flashy effects

### Timing
- Allow 1-2 seconds after each feature reveal
- Pause on CTA screens
