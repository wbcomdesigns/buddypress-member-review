---

## Production Notes

### Music
- Upbeat, modern
- Royalty-free
- Fade under narration

### Text
- Bold, readable at small sizes
- High contrast colors
- Max 5 words on screen

### Pacing
- 15s: Very fast cuts
- 30s: Quick but clear
- 60s: Room to breathe

### Brand Consistency
- Use plugin accent colors
- Include logo in final frame
- Consistent typography
