---

## Positioning

### Problem Statement
BuddyPress communities lack built-in trust signals. Members can't verify who's reliable, skilled, or worth hiring.

### Solution
Member reviews that fit how YOUR community works — whether that's star ratings for marketplaces, testimonials for courses, or endorsements for professional networks.

### Differentiators
1. **Three review modes** — Not locked into star ratings
2. **Verified badges** — Real trust signals, not vanity metrics
3. **BuddyPress-native** — Works with BP, BuddyBoss, Youzify
4. **Privacy controls** — Anonymous option, role restrictions
