Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need the free plugin first?
Yes. Pro is an addon that requires the free base plugin (BuddyPress Member Reviews v3.7.0+). Install free from WordPress.org, then install Pro.
Can I switch between review modes?
Yes. Change modes in Settings > Member Reviews > Pro. Your existing data is preserved — ratings hide in Testimonials mode but reappear if you switch back.
What happens when my license expires?
Pro features keep working. You just stop receiving updates and priority support. Renew anytime to resume updates.
Is there a trial or demo?
No trial, but we offer a 14-day refund policy. If Pro doesn't work for your community, contact support within 14 days.
Does it work with BuddyBoss?
Yes. Fully compatible with BuddyBoss Platform and BuddyBoss Theme, as well as standard BuddyPress and Youzify.
How do I get support?
Pro users get priority email support at support@wbcomdesigns.com. Same-day response during business hours.
