Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with BuddyBoss?
Yes. Fully compatible with BuddyBoss Platform and BuddyBoss Theme, as well as Youzify.
Can members review themselves?
No. The review button doesn't appear on your own profile, preventing fake self-reviews.
Is the free version limited?
No artificial limits. Free includes all core features: ratings, criteria, moderation, notifications, shortcodes.
What's the difference with Pro?
Pro adds Testimonials Mode (text-only), Endorsements Mode (skills), Verified Badges, Flexible Criteria, and REST API.
Can I require approval for reviews?
Yes. Enable in General Settings and reviews stay pending until you approve them.
How do I display top members?
Use [bupr_display_top_members] shortcode anywhere. Customize with max, min_reviews, and layout parameters.
