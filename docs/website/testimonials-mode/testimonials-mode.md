# Testimonials Mode

**Pro Feature** — Requires [BuddyPress Member Reviews Pro](https://wbcomdesigns.com/downloads/buddypress-member-reviews-pro/)

## What is Testimonials Mode?

Testimonials Mode transforms your review system into a text-focused feedback experience. Instead of star ratings, members share authentic stories and experiences in their own words.

## Why Use Testimonials?

Star ratings feel transactional. They reduce complex human experiences to a number between 1 and 5. Testimonials let your community tell real stories.

**What Changes:**
- "Review" becomes "Testimonial" throughout the interface
- Star rating fields are completely hidden
- Aggregate rating displays disappear
- Focus shifts to written experiences and stories

## Perfect For

### Course Platforms
Students share learning journeys and outcomes:
> "This course transformed how I approach web development. The instructor's real-world examples made complex concepts click."

### Coaching & Consulting Sites
Clients describe their success stories:
> "Working with Sarah helped me clarify my business vision. Three months later, I've doubled my client base."

### Membership Communities
Members highlight valuable connections and experiences:
> "The community support here is incredible. I've found collaborators, mentors, and friends who truly understand my journey."

## How to Enable

**Requirements:** This feature requires the [BuddyPress Member Reviews Pro](https://wbcomdesigns.com/downloads/buddypress-member-reviews-pro/) addon.

1. Go to **WordPress Admin > Settings > Member Reviews**
2. Click the **Pro** tab
3. Find **Review Mode** setting
4. Select **Testimonials**
5. Click **Save Changes**

![Pro Features Settings](../images/pro-features-settings-updated.png)

## The Testimonial Flow

1. **Visit a Member Profile** — Click the "Write Testimonial" button
2. **Share Your Experience** — Write about your interaction, results, or impact
3. **Submit** — The testimonial appears on their profile after approval (if moderation is enabled)

## Boost Credibility with Verified Badges

Combine Testimonials Mode with Verified Badges (also a Pro feature) to add trust signals. A verified testimonial carries more weight than an anonymous review.

## Switching Modes Safely

**Note:** Mode switching requires the Pro addon and is not available in the free version.

Your data is safe when switching modes:
- **From Ratings to Testimonials** — Star ratings are hidden but preserved in the database
- **Back to Ratings** — All ratings reappear instantly
- **No Data Loss** — Switch as often as needed for testing

This flexibility lets you experiment with different modes to find what resonates with your community.

## Configuration Tips

**Moderation:** Enable review moderation to curate high-quality testimonials before they go live.

**Email Notifications:** Keep members engaged by notifying them when they receive new testimonials.

**Front-End Submissions:** Let members write testimonials directly on profile pages without visiting the WordPress admin area.
