# Let Members Reply to Reviews

Goal: allow a member to answer a review they received.

## Steps

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Member Reviews** and open the **General** tab.
2. Check that **Owner Replies** is switched on. ("Allow profile owners to respond once to each review they receive.") It is on by default.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

## How a member replies

Tell your members to:

1. Open their own profile and click the **Reviews** tab.
2. Click **Reply to this review** under the review.
3. Write the answer and click **Submit Reply**.

## What members see

- The reply shows under the review with the owner's picture, name, an "Owner Response" label and the date.
- Once a reply is posted, the button is gone. Each review can have one reply.

## Tips

- The reviewer gets a BuddyPress notification about the reply (when **BuddyPress Notifications** is on). No email is sent.
- A reply cannot be edited or removed from the plugin screens.
- Replies are available only on published reviews, and only to the member who received the review. Other members, and administrators, do not get the reply button.
- To stop replies, switch **Owner Replies** off. Existing replies are hidden but kept.

## If it does not work

- The reply button is missing: you must be logged in as the member who received the review, the review must be published, and you must not have replied already. The button shows on the **Reviews** tab list and on the single review page.
- See [Owner Replies](../features/owner-replies.md).
