# Turn On Anonymous Reviews

Goal: let members write a review without their name and picture showing. This helps people give honest feedback.

## Steps

1. Go to **WB Plugins > Member Reviews** and open the **General** tab.
2. Switch **Anonymous Reviews** **on**. ("Allow users to submit reviews anonymously.")
3. Click **Save Changes**.

It is off by default.

## What members see

- The review form gets a tick box: "Send review anonymously."
- When ticked, the review shows the name "anonymous" and a plain default picture instead of the writer's profile. There is no link to the writer's profile.
- The same applies on the single review page, in the activity stream ("An anonymous user", no profile link), in emails and in BuddyPress notifications.

## What you see

Anonymous does not mean unknown to you. In the admin, the **Author** column on the **Reviews** list still shows who wrote it. Only members and visitors are kept in the dark.

## Tips

- Turning the setting off later hides the tick box on the form. Reviews that were already written anonymously stay anonymous on the site.
- Everywhere a review is shown, including the **BP Displayed Member Rating Widget**, an anonymous review shows "Anonymous" and the default picture instead of the reviewer's name and photo.
- Anonymous reviewing can lead to more unfair reviews. Consider switching on approval or reporting too. See [Require approval before reviews go live](require-approval-for-reviews.md).

## If it does not work

- The tick box is missing: check that **Anonymous Reviews** is on and saved, and clear any page cache.
