# BuddyPress Member Reviews - Feature Highlights

---

## Free Features

### Core Rating System

**5-Star Rating System**
The foundation of community trust. Members leave 1-5 star ratings on custom criteria you define.

**Benefit:** Universally recognized rating format that members already understand.

**How it works:** Members visit profiles and click "Leave a Review." They rate on your custom criteria (e.g., Communication, Quality, Professionalism) and write detailed feedback.

**Icon:** ⭐ (Star)

---

**Customizable Rating Criteria**
Define what matters in your community. Create custom rating categories.

**Benefit:** Reviews measure what actually matters to your members, not generic metrics.

**How it works:** Admin sets criteria like "Response Time," "Work Quality," "Helpfulness," or "Teaching Ability." Each criterion gets its own star rating in every review.

**Icon:** ✏️ (Edit/Customize)

---

**Average Rating Display**
Aggregate scores appear on profiles and member directories. Quick trust signals at a glance.

**Benefit:** Members can evaluate someone's reputation in 2 seconds.

**How it works:** Plugin calculates average rating across all reviews and displays it prominently on profiles with total review count.

**Icon:** 📊 (Chart/Average)

---

### Moderation & Safety

**Review Moderation**
Approve or reject reviews before they go live. Keep your community professional.

**Benefit:** Prevent spam, abuse, and inappropriate content before it damages reputations.

**How it works:** Choose "Manual Approval" mode in settings. New reviews enter a moderation queue. Admins approve/edit/delete from WordPress dashboard.

**Icon:** ✅ (Checkmark/Approval)

---

**Review Reporting & Flagging**
Members can flag inappropriate reviews for admin attention.

**Benefit:** Community-powered moderation that scales. Your members help keep reviews honest.

**How it works:** "Report Review" button appears on all reviews. Flagged reviews move to moderation queue with reporter's reason attached.

**Icon:** 🚩 (Flag)

---

**Owner Responses**
Profile owners can reply to reviews publicly. Build dialogue and show accountability.

**Benefit:** Two-way conversation increases engagement. Good businesses respond to feedback.

**How it works:** "Respond to Review" button appears on your own profile's reviews. Responses appear beneath the original review.

**Icon:** 💬 (Speech Bubble)

---

### Privacy & Flexibility

**Anonymous Reviews**
Let members leave honest feedback without revealing their identity.

**Benefit:** Get more candid feedback, especially in sensitive professional situations.

**How it works:** Enable "Allow Anonymous Reviews" in settings. Reviewers can check "Post Anonymously" when writing reviews. Name appears as "Anonymous User."

**Icon:** 🕵️ (Incognito)

---

**Template Overrides**
Customize review display by copying templates to your theme folder.

**Benefit:** Match review design to your brand without editing plugin files. Update-safe customization.

**How it works:** Copy templates from the plugin's `includes/templates/` directory to `/your-theme/review/`. Edit the copied files.

**Icon:** 🎨 (Paint/Design)

---

### Integration & Display

**Activity Stream Integration**
Reviews appear in BuddyPress activity feeds. Turn reviews into social content.

**Benefit:** Automatic promotion of member achievements. Reviews reach your entire community.

**How it works:** When a review is posted (and approved), it automatically appears in the site-wide activity stream and reviewer's activity feed.

**Icon:** 📢 (Megaphone/Broadcast)

---

**Member Directory Ratings**
Display average ratings in member directories.

**Benefit:** Quick trust signals visible when browsing your community.

**How it works:** Rating stars appear next to each member in directory listings, showing their average score at a glance.

**Icon:** 📋 (Directory/List)

---

**Email Notifications**
Automated emails notify members about review activity.

**Benefit:** Keep members engaged even when they're not on your site.

**How it works:** Members receive emails when they get reviewed, when someone responds to their review, or when admins take action.

**Icon:** 📧 (Email)

---

**BuddyPress Notifications**
In-site notifications appear in the BuddyPress notification menu.

**Benefit:** Real-time alerts without leaving your site.

**How it works:** Notification bell shows unread review alerts. Click to see details and navigate to the review.

**Icon:** 🔔 (Bell)

---

### Shortcodes & Widgets

**Top Members Shortcode**
Display highest-rated members anywhere with `[bupr_display_top_members]`.

**Benefit:** Showcase your community's stars on landing pages, homepages, or sidebars.

**How it works:** Add shortcode to any page/post. Shows configurable number of top-rated members with avatars and ratings.

**Parameters:**
- `limit` - Number of members to show
- `criteria` - Which rating criteria to sort by

**Icon:** 🏆 (Trophy)

---

**Top Members Widget**
Sidebar widget showing highest-rated community members.

**Benefit:** Dynamic content that updates automatically as ratings change.

**How it works:** Add widget to any widget area. Configure title, number of members, and display style.

**Icon:** ⚙️ (Widget/Settings)

---

**Recent Reviews Widget**
Sidebar widget showing latest reviews across the community.

**Benefit:** Social proof and activity indicators for visitors.

**How it works:** Add widget to any widget area. Shows most recent reviews with excerpts, member avatars, and ratings.

**Icon:** 🕐 (Clock/Recent)

---

### Compatibility

**BuddyBoss Compatible**
Works seamlessly with BuddyBoss Platform and BuddyBoss Theme.

**Benefit:** Zero configuration needed for BuddyBoss sites. No conflicts, no custom code.

**How it works:** Plugin detects BuddyBoss and automatically adapts to BuddyBoss design patterns and component structure.

**Icon:** ✔️ (Compatibility Check)

---

**BuddyPress 6.0+ Compatible**
Supports all modern BuddyPress versions with regular compatibility updates.

**Benefit:** Future-proof installation. Keep your site updated without breaking reviews.

**How it works:** Regular updates maintain compatibility with latest BuddyPress releases.

**Icon:** 🔄 (Update/Sync)

## Pro Features

### Review Modes

**Ratings Mode** (Pro)
Classic 5-star review system with written feedback. The default mode.

**Benefit:** Traditional, widely-understood review format.

**Use case:** General communities, service directories, freelancer marketplaces.

**Icon:** ⭐ (Star) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

**Testimonials Mode** (Pro)
Text-based recommendations without star ratings. Like LinkedIn recommendations.

**Benefit:** Focus on qualitative feedback rather than numerical scores. More professional tone.

**Use case:** Professional networks, executive communities, coaching platforms.

**How it works:** Members write detailed recommendations. No star ratings appear. Focus shifts to what people say, not what score they give.

**Icon:** 📝 (Text/Writing) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

**Endorsements Mode** (Pro)
LinkedIn-style skill endorsements with one-click validation. Fast and frictionless.

**Benefit:** Zero friction reputation building. Members can endorse skills in seconds.

**Use case:** Professional networks, alumni communities, industry-specific platforms.

**How it works:** Members add skills to their profiles. Other members endorse those skills with one click. Skills display as a tag cloud sized by endorsement count.

**Icon:** 👍 (Thumbs Up) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

### Trust & Verification

**Verified Review Badges** (Pro)
Add visual checkmarks to reviews from verified members.

**Benefit:** Highlight legitimate reviews and reduce trust issues with fake feedback.

**Use case:** Marketplaces, service directories, any community where trust is critical.

**How it works:** Admins mark members as "Verified" (e.g., confirmed customers, certified professionals). Reviews from verified members show a badge.

**Icon:** ✓ (Verified Check) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

### Flexible Rating Systems

**Flexible Rating Criteria** (Pro)
Go beyond stars. Add thumbs up/down, text-only feedback, or weighted criteria.

**Benefit:** Design review systems that match your exact community needs.

**Use case:**
- Thumbs up/down for simple feedback
- Text-only for detailed testimonials
- Weighted criteria where some ratings matter more

**How it works:** Set criteria type (stars/thumbs/text) and weight (importance multiplier). Final rating calculated based on weights.

**Icon:** ⚖️ (Scale/Weight) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

**Weighted Criteria Scoring** (Pro)
Make some rating criteria count more than others in the overall score.

**Benefit:** Prioritize what actually matters. A "Quality" rating can count 2x more than "Speed."

**Use case:** Service marketplaces where quality matters more than speed, tutoring platforms where results matter more than personality.

**How it works:** Assign weight values to each criterion (1-5). Plugin calculates weighted average for overall rating.

**Icon:** 🎯 (Target/Priority) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

### Endorsements Features

**Skills Cloud Display** (Pro)
Visual tag cloud shows member skills sized by endorsement count.

**Benefit:** Instant understanding of what each member is known for. No reading required.

**How it works:** Skills appear as tags on profiles. Most-endorsed skills appear larger. Click a skill to see all members with that skill.

**Icon:** ☁️ (Cloud) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

**One-Click Endorsements** (Pro)
Endorse someone's skill with a single click. No forms, no friction.

**Benefit:** Endorsements lower the barrier to participation. One-click actions encourage more members to contribute.

**Use case:** Building reputation quickly in professional communities.

**How it works:** Click the "+" button next to any skill on a member's profile. Endorsement is instantly recorded and counted.

**Icon:** 👆 (Click/Touch) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

### Display & Content

**Featured Testimonials Shortcode** (Pro)
Display selected testimonials anywhere with `[bupr_featured_testimonials]`.

**Benefit:** Turn member reviews into marketing content for landing pages and sales pages.

**Use case:** Homepage trust sections, about pages, service landing pages.

**How it works:** Admin marks testimonials as "Featured." Use shortcode to display them with custom layouts.

**Parameters:**
- `ids` - Specific testimonial IDs
- `limit` - Number to show
- `style` - Display layout (grid/slider/list)

**Icon:** 🌟 (Featured Star) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

### Developer Features

**REST API Access** (Pro)
Full REST API for querying reviews, ratings, and member statistics.

**Benefit:** Build custom apps, mobile apps, or integrations beyond WordPress.

**Endpoints:**
- `GET /wp-json/bupr/v1/reviews` - List reviews
- `GET /wp-json/bupr/v1/members/{id}/rating` - Member rating
- `POST /wp-json/bupr/v1/reviews` - Submit review

**Use case:** Mobile apps, custom dashboards, third-party integrations.

**Icon:** 🔌 (API/Plugin) + 🏅 (Pro Badge)

---

**30+ Developer Hooks** (Free + Pro)
Actions and filters for complete customization across both the free and Pro versions.

**Benefit:** Modify any behavior without editing plugin files. Build exactly what you need.

**Popular hooks:**
- `bupr_before_review_submit` - Validate reviews
- `bupr_review_rating_calculated` - Modify scoring
- `bupr_can_user_review` - Custom review permissions
- `bupr_review_notification_recipients` - Custom notifications

**Note:** Core hooks are available in the free version. Pro adds additional hooks for endorsements, testimonials, and verified badge workflows.

**Use case:** Membership restrictions, custom workflows, third-party integrations.

**Icon:** 🔧 (Tools/Developer)

## When to Use Each Review Mode

### Ratings Mode (Default)
**Best for:**
- Service marketplaces
- Freelancer platforms
- General communities
- Any site needing quantifiable feedback

**Why:** Universal understanding. Members know how to read and write star reviews.

**Example:** "Sarah rated John 5 stars for Communication and 4 stars for Quality."

---

### Testimonials Mode (Pro)
**Best for:**
- Professional networks
- Executive communities
- Coaching platforms
- B2B communities

**Why:** More professional tone. Focus on qualitative feedback rather than numbers.

**Example:** "Sarah wrote: 'John is an exceptional communicator who delivers high-quality work on tight deadlines.'"

---

### Endorsements Mode (Pro)
**Best for:**
- Professional networks
- Alumni communities
- Skill-based communities
- Industry-specific platforms

**Why:** Zero friction. Members endorse skills in seconds, lowering the barrier to participation.

**Example:** "Sarah endorsed John for: React Development (47 endorsements), Team Leadership (23 endorsements), UI Design (15 endorsements)."
