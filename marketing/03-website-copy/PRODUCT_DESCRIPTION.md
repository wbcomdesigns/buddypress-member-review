## Long Description (WordPress.org Style)

**For WordPress.org Plugin Page:**

```markdown
= Build Trust Through Community Reviews =

BuddyPress Member Reviews adds a complete rating and review system to your BuddyPress or BuddyBoss community. Members can rate and review each other on custom criteria, building reputation profiles that showcase credibility, skills, and expertise.

Perfect for freelancer marketplaces, professional networks, service directories, tutor platforms, alumni communities, and any BuddyPress site where reputation matters.

= Key Features =

**5-Star Rating System**
Members leave 1-5 star ratings with detailed written reviews. Universal format that everyone understands.

**Customizable Rating Criteria**
Create up to 5 custom rating categories. Rate members on Communication, Quality, Reliability, Professionalism, or any criteria specific to your community.

**Review Moderation**
Approve, edit, or delete reviews before they go live. Choose between auto-approval and manual moderation to maintain community standards.

**Owner Responses**
Profile owners can respond to reviews publicly. Build dialogue, address concerns, and thank reviewers for positive feedback.

**Review Reporting & Flagging**
Members can report inappropriate reviews. Flagged reviews enter the moderation queue for admin review.

**Anonymous Reviews**
Allow members to leave reviews without revealing their identity. Perfect for honest feedback in sensitive situations.

**Activity Stream Integration**
New reviews automatically appear in BuddyPress activity feeds, turning reviews into social content that drives engagement.

**Member Directory Ratings**
Display average ratings in member directories. Members' star ratings appear alongside their profiles for quick trust signals.

**Email Notifications**
Members receive email notifications when they get reviewed, when someone responds to their review, or when admins take action.

**BuddyPress Notifications**
In-site notifications appear in the BuddyPress notification bell for real-time alerts.

**Top Members Shortcode**
Use `[bupr_display_top_members]` to showcase your highest-rated members anywhere on your site.

**2 Widgets**
Display top-rated members and recent reviews in sidebars or widget areas.

**Template Overrides**
Customize review display by copying templates to your theme. Update-safe customization without editing plugin files.

**BuddyBoss Compatible**
Works seamlessly with BuddyBoss Platform and BuddyBoss Theme. Zero configuration needed.

= Pro Features =

Upgrade to Pro for advanced review systems:

**3 Review Modes:**
- **Ratings Mode:** Classic 5-star reviews (default)
- **Testimonials Mode:** LinkedIn-style text recommendations without stars
- **Endorsements Mode:** One-click skill endorsements with visual skills cloud

**Verified Review Badges**
Add checkmarks to reviews from verified members. Highlight legitimate feedback and reduce fake review concerns.

**Flexible Rating Criteria**
Go beyond stars. Add thumbs up/down ratings, text-only feedback, or weighted criteria where some ratings count more than others.

**Skills Cloud Display**
Visual tag cloud shows member skills sized by endorsement count. Instantly see what each member is known for.

**Featured Testimonials Shortcode**
Highlight your best testimonials anywhere with shortcodes. Perfect for landing pages and sales pages.

**REST API Access**
Build custom applications, mobile apps, or integrations using the full REST API. Query reviews, ratings, and statistics programmatically.

**30+ Developer Hooks (Free + Pro)**
Customize every aspect of the review system with actions and filters available in both free and Pro versions. Pro adds additional hooks for endorsements, testimonials, and verified badge workflows.

**Priority Support**
Faster response times and dedicated support for Pro customers.

[Get Pro →](https://wbcomdesigns.com/downloads/buddypress-member-reviews/)

= Use Cases =

**Freelancer Marketplace**
Members hire each other for services. Reviews show work quality, communication, and reliability. Top freelancers get more opportunities.

**Professional Network**
LinkedIn-style community where members endorse each other's skills and write recommendations.

**Service Directory**
List consultants, coaches, or service providers. Reviews help visitors find the right expert.

**Tutor Platform**
Students review tutors on teaching quality, patience, and results. Great tutors build reputations that attract students.

**Alumni Network**
Alumni recommend each other for jobs, partnerships, or collaborations. Endorsements become professional references.

**Dating/Matchmaking**
Members vouch for each other after meetups. Verified reviews create safer dating environments.

**Peer-to-Peer Marketplace**
Members buy, sell, or trade with each other. Seller ratings create transaction trust.

= How It Works =

1. **Member visits a profile** and clicks "Leave a Review"
2. **Rates on custom criteria** (e.g., Communication, Quality, Reliability)
3. **Writes detailed feedback** about their experience
4. **Review is submitted** to moderation queue (if enabled) or posted immediately
5. **Average rating updates** on the member's profile and directory listing
6. **Profile owner can respond** to continue the dialogue
7. **Review appears in activity stream** reaching the entire community
```
