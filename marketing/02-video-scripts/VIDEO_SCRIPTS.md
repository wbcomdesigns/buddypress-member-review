## Video Production Notes

### Visual Style Recommendations
- Clean, modern interface shots
- Real plugin screenshots, not mockups
- Smooth transitions between features
- Highlight clicks/interactions with subtle animations
- Use on-screen text for key points
- Keep branding consistent (colors, fonts)

### Voiceover Guidance
- Conversational, not sales-y
- Confident but not arrogant
- Pace: 140-160 words per minute
- Clear pronunciation of "BuddyPress"
- Warm, helpful tone
- Professional, not overly casual

### Music Suggestions
- Upbeat, modern corporate
- Not too loud (voiceover priority)
- Consistent energy throughout
- Avoid cliche stock music tropes
- Fade out cleanly at end

### On-Screen Text Rules
- Use for key points and CTAs
- Don't duplicate voiceover exactly
- Large, readable font
- High contrast
- Animate in/out smoothly
- Never cover important visuals

### End Screen Essentials
Every video should include:
- Plugin logo
- Clear CTA button
- Website URL
- Subscribe prompt (for YouTube)
- Social media links (optional)
- 10 seconds minimum duration

### Call-to-Action Variations

**For free version:**
- "Download Free"
- "Get Started Free"
- "Start Free Today"
- "Install Now"

**For Pro version:**
- "Try Pro - $49/year"
- "Upgrade to Pro"
- "See Pro Features"
- "Get Pro Access"

**For comparison:**
- "Compare Free vs Pro"
- "See All Features"
- "View Pricing"
- "Choose Your Plan"

### Video File Naming Convention
```
BPMR_[Type]_[Duration]_[Version]_[Date].mp4

Examples:
BPMR_SocialAd_15sec_v1_2026-02-12.mp4
BPMR_Explainer_2min_v3_2026-02-12.mp4
BPMR_Testimonial_Maria_30sec_v1_2026-02-12.mp4
```

### Platform-Specific Specs

**YouTube:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Format: MP4
- Add cards at 5-second mark
- End screen: Last 10 seconds

**Facebook/Instagram Feed:**
- Resolution: 1080x1080 (square)
- Aspect ratio: 1:1
- Add captions (85% watch without sound)
- First 3 seconds critical

**Instagram Stories/Reels:**
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Keep text in safe zone
- 15 seconds max for Stories

**Twitter/X:**
- Resolution: 1280x720
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Under 2 minutes 20 seconds
- Add captions

**LinkedIn:**
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Professional tone essential
- Captions recommended
- Square (1:1) performs better in feed

### Accessibility Requirements
- Captions for all voiceover
- High contrast text
- Clear visual demonstrations
- Avoid flashing content
- Provide transcripts
- Audio descriptions for key visuals
