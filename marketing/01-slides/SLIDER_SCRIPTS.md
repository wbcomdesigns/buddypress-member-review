## Comparison Slides (Free vs Pro)

### Comparison 1: Review Modes
**Free Version:**
5-star ratings with custom criteria. Leave reviews with text feedback.

**Pro Version:**
Choose from 3 review modes: Full Ratings (detailed criteria), Testimonials (credibility focus), or Endorsements (quick skill validation). Switch between review modes anytime.

**Upgrade Reason:** Match review style to your community type.

---

### Comparison 2: Verification
**Free Version:**
All reviews accepted (with moderation). No visual distinction between review types.

**Pro Version:**
Verified reviewer badges show authentic reviews. Set verification rules based on friendship, group membership, or custom criteria. Build trust automatically.

**Upgrade Reason:** Prove reviews are from real connections.

---

### Comparison 3: Rating Flexibility
**Free Version:**
Star ratings (1-5) for each criterion. Works for most communities.

**Pro Version:**
Choose stars, thumbs up/down, or text labels per criterion. Assign weights to criteria. Calculate overall scores your way.

**Upgrade Reason:** Match your evaluation style exactly.

---

### Comparison 4: Skills & Endorsements
**Free Version:**
Not available. Reviews only.

**Pro Version:**
LinkedIn-style skills endorsements. One-click endorsements from connections. Visual skills cloud on profiles. Perfect for professional networks.

**Upgrade Reason:** Add professional networking features.

---

### Comparison 5: Display Options
**Free Version:**
Profile tab, 2 widgets, basic shortcode for recent reviews.

**Pro Version:**
Everything in free, plus featured testimonials shortcode, REST API for custom displays, advanced filtering options.

**Upgrade Reason:** Showcase reviews exactly where you want them.
