### Email 3: Getting Results (Day 7)

**Subject:** Tips for getting member reviews

**Preview:** How to encourage participation

**Body:**

Hi [Name],

You've got BuddyPress Member Reviews Pro running. Here's how to get members actually using it.

**Tip 1: Start with endorsements**

If you're not getting many reviews, switch to endorsement mode temporarily. Endorsements require zero writing, so participation jumps. Once members are engaged, you can switch back to full reviews.

**Tip 2: Showcase top-reviewed members**

Use the featured testimonials shortcode to highlight members with great reviews. Recognition motivates more participation.

`[bupr_featured_testimonials count="3"]`

**Tip 3: Enable verified badges strategically**

Don't verify every review. Mark only reviews from confirmed interactions (completed transactions, event attendees, etc.). Selective verification makes badges meaningful.

**Tip 4: Set clear review criteria**

Members leave better reviews when they know what to evaluate. Set criteria specific to your community:

• Professional networks: Expertise, Communication, Reliability
• Learning communities: Helpfulness, Knowledge, Teaching Ability
• Marketplaces: Quality, Timeliness, Professionalism

**Tip 5: Respond to reviews**

When profile owners respond to reviews, it encourages more people to leave them. Enable owner replies in settings.
