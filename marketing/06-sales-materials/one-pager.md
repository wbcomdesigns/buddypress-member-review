## The Problem (2 sentences)

Community sites struggle with credibility - how do you know which members are reliable, skilled, or trustworthy? Without a reputation system, good members go unrecognized and new members don't know who to trust.

---

## 3 Key Benefits

### 1. Build Instant Trust
Member reviews create social proof. New members can see who's highly rated before connecting, hiring, or collaborating. Verified badges prove reviews are authentic.

**Result**: Increase member engagement by 40%+ and reduce disputes from fake profiles.

### 2. Recognize Great Members
Your best contributors deserve recognition. Reviews, ratings, and endorsements make reputation visible - motivating members to stay active and helpful.

**Result**: Top-rated members become community champions and retention improves.

### 3. Works Your Way
Choose from 3 review modes: traditional ratings (5 stars), testimonials (client feedback), or endorsements (LinkedIn-style skills). Pick what fits your community type.

**Result**: Perfect for marketplaces, professional networks, learning platforms, or social communities.

**Q: Can I try before buying Pro?**
A: Yes, start with the free version. It's full-featured for basic reviews. Upgrade when you need advanced features.

**Q: Will it slow down my site?**
A: The plugin uses standard WordPress queries and loads assets only on relevant pages. Test on a staging site to benchmark performance with your setup.

**Q: Is it GDPR compliant?**
A: Reviews are stored as WordPress CPTs. Use WP's built-in privacy tools for data requests.

**Q: Can I migrate from another review plugin?**
A: No built-in import. Reviews are standard WordPress CPT. A developer can write custom migration scripts if needed.
