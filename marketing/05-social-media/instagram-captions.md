## Post Guidelines for Instagram

**Best Practices:**

1. **Image Quality:** Use high-resolution screenshots, mockups, or professional graphics
2. **Consistency:** Maintain brand colors and style across all posts
3. **Carousel Posts:** Break complex features into 3-5 slides for better engagement
4. **Stories:** Repurpose posts as Stories with swipe-up links (if eligible)
5. **Reels:** Create 15-30 second feature demos showing plugin in action
6. **Hashtags:** Use 15-20 relevant hashtags, mix popular and niche tags
7. **Posting Schedule:** Post 3-4 times per week, consistent timing

**Content Mix:**

- 40% Feature highlights and product demos
- 30% Use cases and customer results
- 20% Tips and best practices
- 10% Promotional offers

**Call-to-Action:**

Always end with "Link in bio" and update bio link to match current campaign or priority feature.

**User-Generated Content:**

Encourage customers to:
- Share screenshots of their review implementations
- Tag your account in community success posts
- Use branded hashtag: #BPMemberReviews

**Instagram-Specific Features:**

- Add location tags for WordPress/tech events
- Use poll stickers in Stories for engagement
- Create highlight reels for: Features, Pro Version, Customer Stories, Setup Guides
- Go Live for Q&A sessions about community building
