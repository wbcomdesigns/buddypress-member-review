## Post 9: Tips & Best Practices

**Post:**

5 tips for getting member reviews in your BuddyPress community

Just installing a review plugin isn't enough. Here's how to get members actually using it:

**1. Lead by example**

As admin, review active members first. When people see their profiles getting recognized, they reciprocate.

**2. Make reviews visible**

Add the review widget to profile pages. Use shortcodes to showcase recent reviews on your homepage. Visibility drives participation.

**3. Set specific criteria**

Generic stars don't inspire great reviews. Criteria like "Responsiveness" or "Expertise" give members something concrete to evaluate.

**4. Prompt after interactions**

After successful collaborations or help exchanges, prompt members to leave reviews. Timing matters.

**5. Start with endorsements**

If you're not getting reviews, try endorsement mode (Pro feature). One-click validation is easier than writing full reviews. Build the habit, then introduce detailed reviews.

**Bonus tip:** Respond to reviews as a profile owner. When people see engagement, they're more likely to participate.
