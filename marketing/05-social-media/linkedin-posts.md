## Post 4: Problem/Solution - Anonymous Reviews

**Hook:** Should online communities allow anonymous reviews?

**Body:**

We debated this when building BuddyPress Member Reviews. The arguments:

**Against anonymous reviews:**
• Less accountability
• Easier to abuse
• Lower quality feedback

**For anonymous reviews:**
• Honest feedback in sensitive situations
• Removes fear of retaliation
• More participation in hierarchical communities

We ended up supporting both. Admins can enable or disable anonymous reviews in settings.

**When anonymous reviews work:**

1. Professional communities where junior members need to rate senior members
2. Workplace-style platforms where hierarchy exists
3. Support communities where honest feedback about help quality matters

**When to disable them:**

1. Small communities where accountability matters more than volume
2. Professional networks where attribution builds credibility
3. Marketplaces where verified identity is crucial

**Our approach:**

We included an anonymous review option but made it admin-controlled. Community managers know their context better than we do.

In the Pro version, verified badges help balance anonymous reviews. Even if the reviewer is anonymous, a verified badge shows the interaction was real.

**Bottom line:**

There's no universal answer. But giving community managers the choice respects the diversity of online communities.

What's your take? Do anonymous reviews help or hurt online communities?
