## Tips & How-To

### Post 5: Review Criteria Tip
```
Pro tip: Generic star ratings don't tell the story.

Create review criteria specific to your community:

• Freelance: Quality, Timeliness, Communication
• Learning: Helpfulness, Expertise, Patience
• Professional: Reliability, Skills, Responsiveness

Set custom criteria in BuddyPress Member Reviews settings.
```

### Post 6: Shortcode Tip
```
Want to showcase top-rated members on your homepage?

Use this shortcode:

[bupr_display_top_members total_member="5"]

Displays top-rated members anywhere on your site.

Works with BuddyPress and BuddyBoss.

#WordPress #BuddyPress
```

### Post 7: Moderation Tip
```
Running member reviews in a large community?

Turn on "Require approval" in settings.

Review every submission before it goes live.

Keeps your community professional and spam-free.

BuddyPress Member Reviews includes built-in moderation.

[link]
```

### Post 8: Getting Started Tip
```
Just installed BuddyPress Member Reviews?

Here's how to get your first reviews:

1. Lead by example - review active members
2. Make the form visible (add widget to profiles)
3. Set specific review criteria
4. Prompt members after successful interactions

Make reviews a habit, not an afterthought.
```
